AgentBuddy is a native companion for AI coding agents running on computers you control. Connect from your iPhone or iPad to follow tasks, continue conversations, and review requests while away from your desk.

KEEP YOUR WORK IN VIEW
Organize tasks, projects, and hosts in one place. Open an existing conversation or start a new coding task on a connected host.

FOLLOW THE DETAILS
Read streaming responses, code blocks, images, and command output in a native interface. Keep track of progress without keeping your laptop open.

STAY IN CONTROL
Review approval requests and decide whether to allow or deny an action. Send follow-up instructions as your agent works.

CONNECT YOUR OWN HOSTS
Discover compatible hosts on your local network, pair with your AgentBuddy host, or connect over SSH. Supported agents depend on the software installed and configured on your host.

MADE FOR YOUR WORKFLOW
Use voice input where supported, switch between light and dark themes, and keep your coding conversations close at hand.

A compatible host and a configured coding agent are required. AgentBuddy does not include an AI model subscription; third-party accounts, API access, and charges may apply. AgentBuddy is an independent open-source client for compatible coding agents. Available agents and models depend on your host configuration.

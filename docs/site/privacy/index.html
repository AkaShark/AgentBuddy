隐私政策

本政策适用于 AkaSharker 提供的搭子 AgentBuddy 移动客户端及其任务通知服务。Android 应用包名为 com.akashark.agentbuddy.android。隐私与支持联系邮箱：aaksharker@gmail.com。

AgentBuddy 是连接你自行配置的主机和 AI 服务的远程编程客户端，无需注册独立的 AgentBuddy 账号。你选择的主机运营者、AI 提供商及登录服务会依其各自的政策处理数据；连接别人的主机前，请确认其可信且已获授权。

我们处理哪些数据，以及为什么

  • 连接和本地设置：应用在设备上保存主机地址、配对信息、项目与任务引用、偏好设置，以及你提供的连接凭据，用于恢复连接和显示工作。SSH 凭据使用平台安全存储。部分设置可能进入系统备份，取决于设备与系统配置。
  • 编程与会话内容：你输入的提示、消息、代码、选择的图片或文件，以及任务、命令和审批操作会发送到你连接的主机，并可能由该主机传给所配置的 AI 服务。回复和执行结果返回设备供你查看。主机与 AI 服务可能保存会话；AgentBuddy 的推送代理不接收会话正文。
  • 账号授权：你可通过第三方登录或自行配置的凭据使用 AI 服务。授权结果和账号状态用于所选择的连接与服务；第三方登录页面由对应提供商处理。AgentBuddy 不运营独立的用户注册系统。
  • 语音：使用可选语音功能时，麦克风音频通过语音连接发送至相应的主机或语音服务以生成回复和转写。转写可能成为会话的一部分，并依该主机或提供商的设置保留。
  • 相机：扫码配对使用相机读取连接二维码。Android 的条码识别在设备上运行；相机画面不发送到 AgentBuddy 推送代理。识别结果用于你选择的主机连接。
  • 任务完成通知：启用通知后，服务会处理推送令牌、设备和主机标识、会话及回合标识、完成或失败状态、时间戳，以注册订阅、路由通知、防止重放和重复发送。推送令牌以加密封装经过主机，由部署在 Cloudflare 的通知代理解密后交给 Google Firebase Cloud Messaging（Android）或 Apple APNs（iOS）。通知代理处理连接 IP 等技术信息以防滥用，但不接收提示、代码或会话正文。
  • SDK 技术数据：Android 使用 Firebase Cloud Messaging 与 Google ML Kit 条码扫描。它们可能自动处理安装标识、设备与应用/SDK 版本、配置、功能使用及性能诊断数据，用于投递通知、维护和改进 SDK。没有集成广告 SDK、Firebase Analytics 或 Crashlytics。禁用通知并不等于禁用所有 SDK 技术数据处理。
  • 内容举报：你在应用内主动提交的原因和内容片段会通过 HTTPS 发送给开发者，并由 Cloudflare 存储，用于审阅举报及改进内容安全；不会自动附加完整对话、文件、账号或联系方式。举报记录通常在 30 天内自动清理，网络地址会用于生成每日防滥用标记，不与举报内容一起保存。你可提供举报编号请求提前删除。
  • 支持请求：你主动通过邮箱联系时，我们处理你的邮箱地址及你提供的内容，以答复问题和处理隐私请求。请不要发送密码、私钥或不必要的私人文件。

接收方与数据使用

数据按上述功能传给你选择的主机、AI/语音/身份提供商，以及提供基础设施与通知能力的 Cloudflare、Google 和 Apple。点对点连接在需要时可能使用中继。不同提供商可能在你所在国家或地区之外处理数据。我们不销售个人数据，也不使用它投放广告；必要时可能依法响应有效的法律要求。

权限和选择

相机用于扫码，麦克风用于语音，通知权限用于任务提醒，局域网发现用于寻找主机；某些平台提供可选的悬浮显示及后台连接功能。你可以在系统设置中撤销权限，停止语音，关闭任务通知，断开或移除主机。不授予可选权限不会阻止不依赖该权限的功能。

安全

通知服务使用 HTTPS，SSH 和点对点连接具有各自的加密机制。应用也允许用户手动配置明文 ws:// 地址；这类直接连接不提供传输加密。请优先使用加密连接，不要在不可信网络上通过明文连接传输凭据或私人内容。任何存储或传输方式都不能保证绝对安全。

保留期限

本地数据保留至你移除对应设置、清除应用数据或卸载应用；系统备份可能单独保留。推送订阅按授权期限到期或撤销并清理，事件去重记录目前设置为约 48 小时后到期。防滥用记录、技术日志与支持往来按其服务、安全或法律需要保留，期限可能不同。主机、AI 与平台提供商各自决定其数据保留规则。

数据删除与隐私请求

你可关闭应用内任务通知以撤销相关订阅，并在设备系统设置中清除 AgentBuddy 应用数据或卸载。删除设备数据不会同时删除远程主机、AI 服务或系统备份中的副本，请分别在对应服务中删除会话、文件、授权或备份。

如需请求访问、更正或删除由我们控制的数据，请发送邮件至 aaksharker@gmail.com，主题注明“AgentBuddy 隐私请求”，描述请求及相关通知或支持往来。我们可能请求最少必要的信息以核实归属；请勿提供密码或私钥。若因安全或法律义务需要保留部分数据，我们会说明原因。AgentBuddy 没有独立账号可注销，第三方账号须向该提供商申请删除。

未成年人

未成年人应在父母或监护人指导下使用，并遵守所连接的主机及 AI 服务规定的最低年龄和同意要求。若监护人认为我们处理了未经适当授权的未成年人信息，请通过上述邮箱联系我们以核实并处理删除请求。

更新与相关政策

功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页及更新日期。重要变化会通过适当方式告知。你可查阅 Google 隐私政策、Firebase 隐私信息、ML Kit 条款、Cloudflare 隐私政策及 Apple 隐私政策。你选择的其他服务另有自己的政策。

Privacy Policy

This policy covers the AgentBuddy mobile client (Chinese name: 搭子), provided by AkaSharker, and its task notification service. The Android package is com.akashark.agentbuddy.android. Contact us for support and privacy requests at aaksharker@gmail.com.

AgentBuddy connects to hosts and AI services you configure. It does not require a separate AgentBuddy account. Your host operator and AI, voice and identity providers process data under their own policies. Only connect to hosts you trust and are authorized to use.

Data and purposes

  • Local connection data: host addresses, pairing information, project/task references, preferences and connection credentials are stored on your device to restore connections and display your work. SSH credentials use platform secure storage. Eligible settings may be included in operating-system backups.
  • Content and actions: prompts, messages, code, images/files you select, task actions, commands and approvals are transmitted to your selected host, which may send them to its configured AI services. Responses and execution results return to the device. Hosts and AI services may retain conversations. Our push proxy does not receive conversation content.
  • Authentication: third-party sign-in or credentials you configure authorize the selected service. Authorization results and account status support that connection. The identity provider processes its sign-in page; AgentBuddy does not operate its own account registration system.
  • Optional voice: microphone audio is transmitted to the relevant host or voice service for responses and transcription. Transcripts may be retained as part of a conversation under that host's or provider's settings.
  • Camera: QR pairing scans connection information. Android barcode recognition runs on-device. Camera frames are not sent to our push proxy; decoded connection information is used for the host you select.
  • Notifications: enabled task notifications use push tokens, device/host identifiers, conversation/turn identifiers, completion/failure status and timestamps for subscription management, routing and replay/delivery deduplication. Tokens pass through the host in a sealed form and are decrypted by our Cloudflare-hosted proxy for delivery through Google FCM or Apple APNs. The proxy also processes connection IP addresses for abuse prevention. It does not receive prompts, code or conversation bodies.
  • SDK technical data: Android integrates Google FCM and ML Kit barcode scanning. These SDKs may automatically process installation identifiers, device and app/SDK information, configuration, feature usage and performance diagnostics to deliver notifications and maintain/improve SDK services. We do not integrate advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Disabling notifications does not disable all SDK technical data processing.
  • Content reports: The reason and excerpts you voluntarily submit in the app are sent over HTTPS to the developer and stored by Cloudflare for reviewing reports and improving content safety. No full conversation, files, account or contact details are attached automatically. Reports are normally removed within 30 days. Network addresses derive daily abuse-prevention markers and are not stored with report content. You can request earlier deletion using the report receipt.
  • Support: when you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, private keys or unnecessary private files.

Recipients and choices

Data goes to the hosts and AI/voice/identity services you select and to infrastructure/notification providers Cloudflare, Google and Apple as described above. Peer-to-peer connections may use relays when needed. Providers may process data outside your country. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We may respond to valid legal requirements where necessary.

Camera permission supports QR scanning, microphone permission supports voice, notification permission supports alerts, and local-network access supports host discovery. Some platforms offer optional overlays and background connectivity. Revoke permissions in system settings, stop voice, disable task notifications, or disconnect/remove hosts at any time. Unrelated features remain available without optional permissions.

Security and retention

The notification service uses HTTPS; SSH and peer-to-peer connections use their respective encryption mechanisms. Manually configured ws:// direct connections are also supported and are not encrypted in transit. Prefer encrypted connections and avoid sending credentials or private content over plaintext connections on untrusted networks. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Local data remains until you remove the relevant settings, clear app data or uninstall; system backups may persist separately. Push subscriptions expire or are revoked and cleaned up. Event deduplication records currently expire after approximately 48 hours. Abuse-prevention records, technical logs and support correspondence are retained as needed for their service, security or legal purposes, with potentially different periods. Hosts, AI services and platform providers set their own retention rules.

Deletion and privacy requests

Disable in-app task notifications to revoke related subscriptions. Clear AgentBuddy's data in device settings or uninstall to remove local data. This does not delete copies on hosts, AI services or system backups; delete those conversations, files, authorizations or backups through the respective service.

To request access, correction or deletion of data we control, email aaksharker@gmail.com with the subject “AgentBuddy privacy request” and describe the request and relevant notifications or correspondence. We may request the minimum information needed to verify ownership; do not send passwords or private keys. We will explain any data that must be retained for security or legal obligations. There is no separate AgentBuddy account to delete; contact third-party providers about their accounts.

Minors and changes

Minors should use the app with a parent or guardian's guidance and meet the minimum-age and consent requirements of their selected host and AI services. Parents or guardians who believe we processed a minor's information without appropriate authorization may contact us to investigate and request deletion.

We update this page and its date when our practices change and communicate material changes as appropriate. See the provider policies linked above.
